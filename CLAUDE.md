## Changelog fragments

A non-test change under `tinyagentos/` or `desktop/src/` requires a changelog
fragment in the same PR — `changelog.d/<pr>-<slug>.md` or
`changelog.d/tsk-<cardid>-<slug>.md` — or a `CHANGELOG.md` line.
The single-source rule lives in [`docs/changelog-fragments.md`](docs/changelog-fragments.md)
and is summarized in [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) (the "Documentation gate" section).
