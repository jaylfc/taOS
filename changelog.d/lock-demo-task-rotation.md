### Added
- The lock screen's DEMO agent islands now rotate their "current task" while the screen is unlocked, staggered a few seconds apart in a round-robin so islands never appear to move all at once. The status text animates with a slide/fade transition (a crossfade under `prefers-reduced-motion`) and a brief highlight in the agent's own colour; a completed step ("✓ ...") gets a green tint and a shorter dwell. The `/auth/lock-widgets` poll now schedules its next fetch from the server's `refresh_in_ms` instead of a fixed 15s interval, and pauses while the screen is off or the app is backgrounded.
