- Notifications: per-person delivery controls (#206). Each user now has, per notification source, a delivery style (banner / alert / none) and a Notification Centre visibility toggle alongside the existing mute, plus a personal Do Not Disturb window that is evaluated in their own timezone (the Settings panel sends the browser's IANA zone with each save). Alert toasts stay until dismissed; style "none" is stored with no pop-up; centre-off notifications are still stored but filtered out of the Centre view, so nothing is lost. Broadcast notifications apply each recipient's own mute, style and Do Not Disturb settings before live delivery (SSE and web-push). Delivery preferences and Do Not Disturb moved from global, admin-only settings to per-user rows keyed on the authenticated caller; pre-existing global prefs are preserved under a legacy ownerless key and no longer apply to anyone. Follow-ups: per-source notification preview controls and notification sounds are not part of this change.
