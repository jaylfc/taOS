Notifications: per-person delivery controls (#206). Each user now has, per notification source, a delivery style (banner / alert / none) and a Notification Centre visibility toggle alongside the existing mute, plus a personal Do Not Disturb window that is evaluated in their own timezone (the Settings panel sends the browser's IANA zone with each save). Alert toasts stay until dismissed; style "none" is stored with no pop-up; centre-off sources are toast-only and never stored (unmuted OS-originated notifications excepted: they always store, because their idempotency record commits with the row). Delivery preferences and Do Not Disturb moved from global, admin-only settings to per-user rows keyed on the authenticated caller; pre-existing global prefs are preserved under a legacy ownerless key and no longer apply to anyone.
