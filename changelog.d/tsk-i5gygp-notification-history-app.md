### Added
- Notification History app: a standalone, searchable window for past notifications. Search by text, filter by source, level, date range (Today, 7 days, 30 days, All, or a custom from/to) and state (All, Unread, Archived), page back with Load more, and click a row to read its full message. Find it from desktop search or the "View history" link in the notification panel footer.
### Changed
- The Notifications app drops its Archive tab and stays the live inbox plus preferences. Its footer link and the notification panel footer now say "View history" and open Notification History; a legacy Archive dock pin opens Notification History too.
