### Added
- `llama-swap` backend type: a local, OpenAI-compatible type a worker probes when configured with `TAOS_EXTRA_BACKENDS="llama-swap=http://127.0.0.1:8080"`. It serves chat, embeddings and reranking, reports `models` from `/v1/models` and `loaded_models` from `/running` (also in `/api/models/loaded` for a `llama-swap` provider), is told apart from plain llama.cpp by `/running`, and is not reported twice when it sits on a `llama-cpp`/`vllm` default port. Its per-model unload (`POST /api/models/unload/<model>`) is registered as a residency backend in `tinyagentos/backend_unload.py` for the GPU work queue to call; nothing calls it yet (#3229).
