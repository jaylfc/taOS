### Fixed

- On mobile, the top bar no longer shows the taOS wordmark, which sat under the handset's front camera hole; the status indicators stay centred and the search and notification buttons move in from the rounded screen corner.
- On mobile, notification toasts are back at the top of the screen, full width just below the top bar.
- The handset nav bar's home button now takes taOS to its home screen and backgrounds the open app (it stays in the switcher), through the F13 key the PWA listens for on mobile.
- In the installed mobile app, the solid dark bar behind the top bar is gone: the wallpaper shows through again, with only a 1px strip at the very top edge.
