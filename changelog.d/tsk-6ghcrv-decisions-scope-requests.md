- The Decisions app now lists pending agent scope requests beside access requests, showing the agent, requested scopes, requested project (labelled as requested, never as the binding) and the effective binding ("Grants OS-wide (all projects)" whenever no project scope is granted), reason and age. Approve grants exactly the ticked subset of the requested scopes; Deny requires a reason, which the deny route now records as `deny_reason` (new column, migrated on startup) and returns from the scope-request read routes. The scope-request list loads independently of the decisions inbox, and the deny reason box is capped at 2000 characters to match the route.
