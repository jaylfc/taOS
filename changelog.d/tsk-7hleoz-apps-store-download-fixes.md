### Fixed

- apps-store downloads now land under `<sha256[:16]>-<basename>` and assertions prove the hashed name; `durable_replace` is promoted off the event loop via `asyncio.to_thread`; temp files are cleaned up on `BaseException` (including cancellation); `fetch_manifest` runs its blocking verify/accept/cache inside `to_thread` under the serialising lock; manifest relative-URL segments are validated with `fullmatch` so a trailing newline is rejected; and the fixed 300 s total download deadline is replaced by a per-read idle timeout (chosen over scaling the total cap) so a stalled stream cannot hold the temp file and the caller indefinitely.
