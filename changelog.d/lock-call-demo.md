- Lock screen: an incoming-call demo in the live zone. A call from Naira gathers
  the showing islands or cards into one liquid droplet that stretches into a
  call card, with Decline, Voicemail, Answer and a headline Send to PA. With
  Send to PA, the PA's conversation plays as a live transcript that reveals
  word by word, with Take over and End call pinned for the whole call. When the
  PA finishes, the card shrinks to a one-line pill, the feed returns exactly as
  it was, and a "New event added · Call Naira · 5:30 pm" notification appears
  at the top of Alerts.
- Lock screen: the call is scripted demo content served by `/auth/lock-call`
  (with `/ring`, `/reset` and `/action`). All four routes are console-only and
  404 unless `TAOS_LOCK_DEMO_CALL` is set, which is independent of the other
  demo flags. There is no path from it to a phone line, contacts or a calendar.
