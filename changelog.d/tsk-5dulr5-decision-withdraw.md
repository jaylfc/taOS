### Added
- Decisions: the asker can now withdraw a decision that went moot before it was answered. `POST /api/decisions/{id}/withdraw` with `{"reason": "..."}` works for the asking agent (its own registry token, `decisions_write` on the decision's project) or the decision's owner/admin session; any other caller gets 404, an empty reason 400, and anything not `pending` 409 (answered, superseded and withdrawn are terminal). The record is kept with its reason under `status=withdrawn`, leaves the pending inbox, shows under a new Withdrawn tab in the Decisions app, and each withdraw writes a governance audit entry. Existing databases gain the `withdraw_reason`, `withdrawn_at` and `withdrawn_by` columns on startup (tsk-5dulr5).
