### Added
- Every chat turn is now also ingested into taOSmd with its role, session id, turn index and session date, so the agent's recall can rebuild the surrounding conversation on any host, including CPU-only ones. Covers chat (HTTP and WebSocket), channel hub inbound and outbound, and bridge session replies. Each turn goes to the own shelf of every agent in the conversation, and a person's own messages also to the personal memory shelf of the signed-in person who wrote them. Delivery runs in the background from a saved queue that retries, keeps what it could not deliver, and does not send a delivered turn again. Each send carries a source id, so it is retry-safe once taOSmd honours source ids; until then a crash or lost reply during a send can store one turn twice, and turns left in flight by a crash are closed out as delivered rather than sent again.
- `python -m tinyagentos.turn_backfill --data-dir <dir>` queues existing chat history for taOSmd; the running server delivers it. It is resumable and rate limited, and on an install with several people it skips turns written by the old shared `user` id instead of guessing whose they were.
