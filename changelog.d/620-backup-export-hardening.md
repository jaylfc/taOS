### Changed

- **Backup/export bundle hardening (#620)** — the export bundle's secret and privacy handling is stricter: webhook URLs are now redacted (a `url` under `webhooks` is replaced wholesale, and Discord/Slack webhook URL shapes are masked wherever they appear, since those URLs are credentials); the sensitive-key matching also covers the whole words `key`, `pin`, `hash` and `salt` (`FOO_KEY`, `MY_PIN`, `pin_hash`, `*_salt`) while whole-word matching keeps lookalikes like `keyboard_layout` untouched; absolute paths in `taosmd_dir` and agent `workspace` fields are rewritten relative to the data dir (or reduced to their final component) instead of leaking the host layout; the manifest now carries `contains_all_users_data: true` and the Settings export control warns that the bundle holds every user's data; and the serialized bundle is capped at 64 MiB, with the export endpoint refusing an oversized bundle with a clear 413 error instead of serving it unbounded.
