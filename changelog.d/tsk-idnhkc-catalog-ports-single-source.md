### Fixed
- Catalog ports duplication (lib-audit R2-34): 27 service manifests carried the same port list under both ``requires.ports`` and ``install.ports``; ``install.ports`` was the dead branch and on qdrant/mailserver it held ``"host:container"`` strings that crashed ``int()`` at install. ``install.ports`` is now the single source of truth, every duplicated manifest was migrated (one source of truth remains, with bare ints coerced to ``"host:container"`` strings), and ``docker_installer`` parses the string form and emits a correct ``<allocated_host>:<container>`` mapping per declared port. ``scripts/audit-manifests.py`` flags any future manifest that re-introduces the duplication. Linux Docker Engine also does not resolve ``host.docker.internal`` by default, so when a service's env references that hostname (perplexica, open-webui) the installer now adds ``extra_hosts: ["host.docker.internal:host-gateway"]`` and the container can reach the host.
