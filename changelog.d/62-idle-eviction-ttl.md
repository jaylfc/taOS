### Added

- Scheduler: TTL-based idle eviction for resident models (taOS #62). `LoadedModel` gains a workload `model_class` (image_gen, stt, tts, embedding, reranker, query_expansion, chat_small, chat_large, reasoning) and an optional per-model `ttl_seconds` override; defaults follow the issue table (embedding 30s, reranker 15s, image-gen 5 min, chat ≤4B 10 min, chat ≥7B / reasoning 30 min). `CoreAwareModelScheduler.sweep_idle()` evicts residents idle past their TTL — pinned / always_resident models and `ttl_seconds=0` never expire, and the clock runs from `last_used_at`, so a model in use stays resident. The sweep is driven by the backend catalog's existing poll loop (`BackendCatalog.attach_idle_sweeper()`), which `create_app` points at the scheduler, so idle eviction runs in the live process without the scheduler owning a background task; the sweep re-checks expiry immediately before each eviction so a model touched mid-sweep is never evicted. Pressure eviction (LRU under load) and pinning already existed and are unchanged.
