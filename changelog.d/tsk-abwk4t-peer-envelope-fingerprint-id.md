### Fixed
- Peer envelopes now carry one identity form end to end: `from` and `to` are `hub:<signing-key fingerprint>`, the same key friend-accept writes to `contacts.contact_id`. Before, `build_envelope` stamped `hub:<username>` on both fields while the inbox compared them against fingerprint-keyed rows, so every real envelope between two nodes was refused (403 on `from`, or 503 "hub identity not configured" when the node had no hub_authors row). `local_contact_id()` replaces the hub_authors lookup for the recipient check, `send_handshake`/`build_envelope` take `to_contact_id`, collab invites no longer require a `hub_username` to deliver, a contact row not keyed on its own pinned key is refused with 403, and the collab-invite Decisions question names the inviter by the contact row's display name instead of the 64-hex id. New two-node test harness `tests/helpers/two_nodes.py` (tsk-abwk4t).
