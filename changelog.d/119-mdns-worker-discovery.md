### Added
- mDNS worker discovery (#119): the controller now also publishes a `_tinyagentos._tcp.local.` service (alongside `taos.local`) whose TXT records carry its URL, hub identity fingerprint, and hardware tier (`tinyagentos/services/mdns_publisher.py`, wired in `app.py`). Workers browse for it with the new `tinyagentos.worker.discovery` module: `python -m tinyagentos.worker.pair` accepts an omitted controller URL (scan, pick, manual-entry fallback), `python -m tinyagentos.worker.discovery --pick` prints a chosen controller URL for scripts, and `scripts/install-worker.sh` may be run interactively without a URL and resolves one at pairing time. `--expect-fingerprint` on both CLIs selects a specific controller without prompting. Discovery is LAN-only; remote attach still uses pairing codes + mesh.
